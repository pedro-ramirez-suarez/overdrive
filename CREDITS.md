# Credits & Third-Party Assets

OVERDRIVE is built with free, redistributable assets. Only the map data behind
the Tres Marías track carries an attribution requirement; the rest is credited
because crediting the creators is the right thing to do.

## Engine

- **Godot Engine** 4.7 — © Juan Linietsky, Ariel Manzur and contributors.
  Licensed under the MIT License. <https://godotengine.org>

## Vehicle models

- **Free Low Poly Vehicles Pack** by Raphael Gonçalves (Rgsdev) —
  **CC0 1.0 / public domain**. <https://www.patreon.com/rgsdev>
  Used for the bulk of the car roster.

- **Car Kit** by Kenney — **CC0 1.0 / public domain**. <https://kenney.nl>
  Used for the remainder of the car roster.

Both packs are CC0: free to use, modify and redistribute, for any purpose,
including commercial, with no attribution required.

## Map data

Two tracks are real places, and their layouts and hills come from open data:

- **Tres Marías** — the Bulevar Alfredo Zalce circuit around Ciudad Tres Marías
  in Morelia, Michoacán.
- **Nordschleife** — the Nürburgring Nordschleife in the Eifel, Germany.

- Road and circuit geometry from **OpenStreetMap** — © OpenStreetMap
  contributors, made available under the **Open Database Licence (ODbL 1.0)**.
  <https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright>
  These tracks are produced works derived from that data, so the attribution
  above must travel with any build that ships them.

- Ground heights from the **NASA/USGS Shuttle Radar Topography Mission** 1
  arc-second dataset (SRTM), courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey —
  **public domain**. <https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/products/srtmgl1v003/>

## Everything else

All track pieces, scenery, terrain, road textures, UI, sounds and game code were
made for this project and are released under the MIT License (see `LICENSE`).
